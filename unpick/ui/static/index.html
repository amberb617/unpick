Offline AI add-on checker

Is this AI add-on safe to install?

Skills, rules files, MCP servers (small programs that give your AI extra tools) and plugins can carry instructions your AI follows but you never see. unpick reads them the way your AI does, and tells you whether to install.

A GitHub link, npm:package or pypi:package is downloaded into quarantine (a locked folder your AI cannot load) and never run. For a folder on this computer, Mac: select it in Finder and press Option-Command-C. Windows: Shift + right-click it and choose "Copy as path".
